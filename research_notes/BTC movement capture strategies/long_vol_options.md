# Direction-Agnostic Long-Volatility Option Strategies on BTC (short-dated focus)

Research date: 2026-10-15. About 17 tool calls. The SSRN paper by Wen & Zhang returned HTTP 403, so it was not read. Several "guru" pages were found only through search snippets and are marked as such.

## 1. Long straddles / strangles on BTC daily/weekly expiries: when recommended, entry rules, strikes, exits

### Takeaway
Sources push long straddles and strangles in three situations: when IV is compressed or cheap in absolute terms, ahead of macro catalysts (CPI, FOMC, elections), and after a long squeeze or low-vol regime. I found **no published, cost-inclusive rule set with proven positive expectancy for systematically buying BTC dailies**. Most of the systematic evidence says the other side, selling short-dated BTC vol, is the profitable one on average. Long vol only pays off in rare large-move windows.

### Cited Findings
- **Absolute-IV-cheap framing (2025-12):** Deribit's weekly analytics reports (week 50-52, 2025) say BTC IV had compressed to the high-30s/low-40s, printing lows, and was "cheap vol in absolute terms" per Deribit's CCO. They call long-vol structures such as straddles "an appealing trade" ahead of CPI prints and Fed speeches. The same reports also note that BTC "continues to provide a positive carry (volatility risk premium)", meaning short vol still had the edge. — [Deribit Insights, Week 51 2025](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/crypto-derivatives-analytics-report-week-51-2025/); [Week 50](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/crypto-derivatives-analytics-report-week-50-2025/); [Week 52](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/crypto-derivatives-analytics-report-week-52-2025/) (content from search snippets)
- The same reports say the recent FOMC meeting and a subdued US CPI "failed to ignite substantial market movements", so the events did not produce the moves that long vol needed. — [Deribit Insights Week 50/51 2025](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/crypto-derivatives-analytics-report-week-51-2025/) (snippet)
- **Event pricing example:** BTC options priced a **1.98% move** for the Oct 14–15 window around a US CPI release. IV for the CPI window was **37.9%**, against **34.8%** afterward, so an event premium of about 3 vol points was baked in. — [KuCoin News flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/bitcoin-options-price-1-98-move-ahead-of-us-cpi-report) (aggregator, snippet only)
- **Mean-reversion / low-vol entry (2020):** Deribit's Ulla Rone (Oct 24, 2020) recommended straddles and strangles for "first time volatility traders" after BTC IV hit YTD lows, using the mean-reverting nature of vol and the US election as the catalyst. The article gives no entry/exit rules, strike guidance or cost numbers. — [Deribit Insights "BTC Trading Uncertainty"](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/trading-uncertainty/)
- **2026 regime:** BTC 30-day realized vol was about 21.8% annualized while 30-day IV stayed near 36%, a large IV-over-RV gap, which makes long-vol carry expensive. — [ByteFederal news](https://www.bytefederal.com/news/why-bitcoin-options-stay-expensive-as-volatility-hits-2026-lows/41092) (snippet; secondary). In 2025, 30-day IV fell from about 70% to about 45% as institutions sold covered calls. — [CoinDesk, 2025-12-31](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/12/31/bitcoin-market-calmed-in-2025-thanks-to-yield-hungry-institutions)
- **Short-dated compression (Aug 18, 2026):** BTC 7-day RV was 19% (lowest since Sep 2025) and 7-day IV was about 23% (lowest since 2023). That is still about a 4-point IV premium even at record-low levels. Block Scholes describes a June–September "summer lull". — [Block Scholes Substack](https://blockscholesresearch.substack.com/p/bitcoins-short-dated-implied-volatility)
- **Squeeze entry (technical, not options-tested):** A BB/Keltner squeeze breakout backtest on BTC spot (2020–2025) reports Sharpe 1.03 and +227.8% for the best of **243 parameter combos**, with short BB periods (5–7 days) working best. This is a directional spot-breakout test chosen in-sample, not a straddle test, so it is heavily overfit-prone. — [PyQuantLab / Medium](https://pyquantlab.medium.com/optimizing-the-bollinger-band-keltner-channel-squeeze-strategy-volatility-breakout-trading-in-70b49101cb30). Generic educators suggest buying an ATM straddle "if you think the squeeze phase is ending", but give no BTC data. — [Option Samurai blog](https://optionsamurai.com/blog/bollinger-bands-strategy/)
- **Strike mechanics:** A long straddle is ATM call plus ATM put (net delta about 0). It becomes directional as price moves away from the strike, which is why practitioners hedge it with futures/perps. The higher the premium, the larger the move needed to break even. — [Deribit Insights, How to Use Delta Hedging to Lock up Profits](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/how-to-use-delta-hedging-to-lock-up-profits/); [Deribit Position Builder primer](https://insights.deribit.com/market-research/deribit-position-builder-primer-on-basic-option-strategies/); [Deribit multi-leg straddles/strangles primer](https://insights.deribit.com/education/multi-leg-options-positions-part-1-straddles-and-strangles/)
- **Delta Exchange (India):** It lists BTC/ETH daily, weekly and monthly options, plus exchange-native **"MOVE"/straddle contracts** (a single contract equal to an ATM straddle), INR-settled. Its educational blog describes the long straddle as a play for when you "believe BTC prices will undergo volatility but aren't sure what direction". — [Delta Exchange straddle/MOVE markets](https://www.delta.exchange/app/move_options/markets); [Delta blog: Long Straddle](https://www.delta.exchange/blog/understanding-long-straddle-options-trading-strategies)
- **Delta Exchange community content leans short-vol:** Delta's own featured algo, "2860% returns in 2 years" (posted Jan 5, 2024), is a **short** BTC daily straddle sold at 11:30 IST, about 6h before expiry. Over Jan 2022–Dec 2023 it ran 723 trades with a 66.5% win rate, profit factor 1.61, about $29 average fee per trade with fees included, and a max DD of more than $2,500 on $5k start (compounded). The long side of this same daily trade loses by construction. — [Delta Exchange blog](https://www.delta.exchange/blog/the-algo-trading-strategy-which-made-2860-returns-in-the-past-2-years). Tradetron hosts a marketplace of Delta-linked option algos. — [Tradetron marketplace](https://tradetron.tech/strategies?exchanges%5B%5D=DELTA)

### Inferences
- Explicit exit rules (profit target as a % of debit, trailing stop, time stop) for long BTC daily straddles were not found in any reputable source. Rules seen in retail content (e.g. take profit at +50–100% of debit, exit before the final hours to avoid theta) are generic and unbacked by BTC data. A backtest would have to establish them from scratch.
- The strongest-evidenced long-vol entry filter is **relative cheapness**: IV near or below recent RV, or a near-zero event premium, rather than chart squeezes. In 2025–2026 IV mostly stayed above RV even at record lows, which is a headwind for any always-on long-straddle rule.
- The asymmetry is clear in the data. Delta's short-daily backtest has a win rate of about 2/3, so a long daily straddle wins about 1/3 of days and must capture outsized moves to pay for the rest.

### Gaps
- No published BTC long-daily-straddle backtest with Bollinger/Keltner or ATR filters applied to *option* P&L (as opposed to spot) was found.
- No source gave the distribution of BTC moves around the US cash open, or specifically for ETF-flow days, in a long-straddle context.
- Tony Stewart's (ex-Morgan Stanley BTC vol desk) Deribit "Option Flows" columns discuss gamma demand and fast money buying dailies. No systematic rules or returns were extracted. — [Deribit Option Flows index](https://insights.deribit.com/option-flows/)

## 2. Gamma scalping BTC (long options + perp delta hedging)

### Takeaway
Gamma scalping is long options plus delta-hedging with perps/futures at fixed price or delta thresholds. It profits only if realized vol exceeds the implied vol paid plus hedging and option-spread costs. In BTC, short-dated ATM option spreads (about 200–300 bp of premium) dwarf perp hedge costs (about 1–5 bp), so most of the friction comes from the option entry and exit. The only published numeric BTC example underperformed holding the coin.

### Cited Findings
- **Mechanics:** Buy an ATM straddle (delta about 0). When BTC rises $1,000, delta might be +0.30, so you sell 0.30 BTC perp. When it falls $1,000, delta might be -0.20, so you buy 0.20. Each re-hedge locks in a slice of the move. Deribit's app offers one-click delta hedging. — [Deribit Insights, Delta hedging to lock up profits](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/how-to-use-delta-hedging-to-lock-up-profits/); [Greeks.live gamma scalping guide](https://prime.greeks.live/guide/a-guide-to-gamma-scalping-in-crypto-markets/); [MenthorQ crypto gamma scalping guide](https://menthorq.com/guide/gamma-scalping-in-crypto-markets/) (search snippets)
- **Costs:** Futures spreads are about 1–5 bp depending on expiry, while short-term ATM option spreads typically run **200–300 bp**. Gamma hedging is more expensive than plain delta hedging, though perps minimize hedge cost. — [Panoptic × Block Scholes research](https://panoptic.xyz/research/panoptic-block-scholes-research-gamma-scalping) (snippet). One practitioner example assumes about 0.02% round-trip slippage per hedge and about 0.01%/day funding on perp hedges. — [crypto-trading-pro guide](https://crypto-trading-pro.contentwave.net/article/deltaneutral-options-market-making-for-btc-eth-2026-guide) (low-quality source, colour only)
- **Published BTC result:** The Panoptic/Block Scholes simulation delta-hedged a 10-delta monthly long call on a WBTC-USDC 30 bp pool. Gamma scalping netted about **+12%**, while buy-and-hold returned **+105%**. Volatile days triggered up to **66 re-hedges in one day**. Low LP supply made long convexity ("streamia") expensive in BTC, unlike their ETH pool, where scalping beat buy-and-hold. — [Panoptic × Block Scholes Part II](https://panoptic.xyz/research/panoptic-block-scholes-research-gamma-scalping) (DeFi perpetual options, not Deribit; undated in fetch)
- **Academic:** "Delta hedging bitcoin options with a smile" (Quantitative Finance, 2023) studies hedge-ratio choice for BTC options. Smile-adjusted deltas matter for hedge efficiency. — [Tandfonline](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14697688.2023.2181205) (abstract-level only)
- **Fees are the practical killer:** A rule that is profitable on mid prices turns negative once a spread and commission are charged on every leg and every re-hedge. — [dependability.us, "Gamma Scalping in Practice", 2026-05-23](https://dependability.us/articles/2026-05-23-gamma-scalping/) (snippet; generic, not BTC-specific)
- **Open-source lab:** A GitHub repo implements discrete-rebalancing delta hedging with vol mismatch and transaction costs, plus a BTC short-vol backtest on Deribit data. It could serve as a starting point for a long-gamma variant. — [GitHub Delta-Hedging-Lab](https://github.com/subhanaamirr/Delta-Hedging-Lab)

### Inferences
- On BTC dailies, gamma P&L is about ½·Γ·(ΔS² − σ_imp²·S²·Δt). Hedging mostly changes the *path* of P&L (it locks in gains from intraday reversals), not its sign. The expected sign is still set by RV − IV, and the VRP evidence below says that is negative on average.
- Hedge-trigger choice (e.g. re-hedge every ±1% move or ±0.1 delta) trades off hedge fees against path variance. Perp taker fees on Delta India (0.05% + 18% GST) are 10× option-notional fees, so frequent re-hedging there is costly.

### Gaps
- No practitioner-reported live P&L for BTC gamma scalping on Deribit or Delta (with fees) was found.
- Greeks.live and MenthorQ guides were not fully read, only search snippets.

## 3. Published backtests / performance data for systematic long BTC straddles/strangles and "buy vol before events"

### Takeaway
The systematic evidence for 2019–2025 says **BTC options are on average overpriced (positive VRP)**. Weekly short ATM straddles and short weekend strangles made money after fees, so the mirror long trades lost. The one published "buy vol before FOMC/CPI" test was positive but had fewer than 15 trades. Buying the single structurally weakest short slot (Sunday strangles) earned only about 2.1% APR.

### Cited Findings
- **Amberdata / Deribit Insights (Jan 24, 2023; data Apr 2019–Dec 2022):** 30-day IV exceeded RV about **70%** of the time. The VRP averaged about **+15 vol points in contango**, and the term structure was in contango 77.5% of the time (mean backwardation spell 85 hours). Weekly short ATM straddles, delta-rehedged on a 2.5% move, were "consistently profitable despite crashes"; the best mix was 90% weekly straddles plus 10% spot. Butterflies underperformed straddles. **Event long-vol:** buying ATM *calls* 7 days before FOMC/CPI and closing 1 day before the event (to avoid post-event IV crush) was positive, but on **fewer than 15 trades each**, "too early to conclude". The study says long vol "performs poorly systematically". — [Deribit Insights: Finding edge in four years of volatility regimes](https://insights.deribit.com/industry/bitcoin-options-finding-edge-in-four-years-of-volatility-regimes/)
- **Weekend vol backtest (Cryptarbitrage, Deribit Insights, May 21, 2025; data 2020-01-01 to 2025-04-30):** Sell 0.35-delta BTC strangles Friday 16:00 UTC, expiring Sunday 08:00 UTC, held to expiry. Costs were included: Deribit fee of 0.0003 BTC per option plus **5% slippage on premium**. Result: 247 trades, 202 wins, +1.548 BTC (+154.8%), **29.7% APR**, max DD 23.8%. Selling the Sunday-to-Monday slot lost **−0.922 BTC (−17.7% APR)**, yet *buying* Sunday strangles returned only **about 2.1% APR**. The author warns that a losing short side does not imply a profitable long side. — [Deribit Insights: Option Backtest – Selling Weekend Vol, Revisited](https://insights.deribit.com/education/option-backtest-selling-weekend-vol-revisited/); original version: [Selling Weekend Vol](https://insights.deribit.com/education/option-backtest-selling-weekend-vol/)
- **Academic VRP (Almeida, Grith, Miftachov, arXiv 2410.15195, 2024; data Jul 2017–Dec 2022, 5.38M Deribit trades):** BTC variance risk premium about **0.14 annualized** for a 27-day horizon (0.12 in the high-vol regime, 0.17 in the low-vol regime). The VRP is generally positive and turned negative only in **Mar 15 – Apr 8, 2020** (COVID crash). Unlike the S&P, the BTC VRP is driven mainly by **upside** return states. The VRP is *higher* in low-vol regimes, so "IV looks cheap" periods were, on average, still poor for buyers. — [arXiv: Risk Premia in the Bitcoin Market](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.15195v2); related: [Grith et al., Option-Implied Risk Premia and Crypto Market Regimes](https://bcf.princeton.edu/published-papers/option-implied-risk-premia-and-cryptocurrency-market-regimes)
- **Equity analogue:** Zero-beta ATM straddles lose about 3% per week on average, and delta-hedged option returns are unconditionally negative. — cited in search summary of [Quantpedia VRP effect](https://quantpedia.com/strategies/volatility-risk-premium-effect) (snippet)
- **Methodological caution:** Wen & Zhang (SSRN 7327512) analyze 49,558 daily inverse BTC-option strategy-date positions on Deribit. They focus on transaction completion and sample-selection bias in backtests, meaning quoted mids or untraded strikes inflate results. — [SSRN abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7327512) (403 on fetch; snippet only)
- Data/tooling for building one's own test: Block Scholes strategy backtester ([link](https://console.blockscholes.com/products/strategy-backtest)) and free Deribit options OHLCV ([CryptoDataDownload](https://www.cryptodatadownload.com/data/deribit/)).

### Inferences
- The best-supported long-vol niches are (a) short pre-event holding windows that exit *before* the release, capturing IV run-up rather than the move, and (b) crisis tails (March 2020-type). Neither has a large enough sample to call it a proven edge.
- Because the BTC VRP is upside-driven, an OTM-call-heavy "strangle" may be less overpriced than ATM puts in some regimes. This is speculative and untested here.
- Any BTC long-daily-straddle backtest should charge realistic friction: 5% of premium slippage (Cryptarbitrage) or the 200–300 bp ATM spread (Block Scholes), plus fees. Profits computed on mids should be treated as overstated (Wen & Zhang).

### Gaps
- I found no published systematic **long** BTC daily (0–2 DTE) straddle or strangle backtest with numbers.
- No post-2023 update of the Amberdata FOMC/CPI long-vol test was found.
- There was no data on whether ETF-flow days or US-open hours carry a negative intraday VRP.

## 4. Named practitioners/firms, costs, and Indian community

### Takeaway
Institutional voices (Amberdata, Block Scholes, Deribit research, QCP) mostly document a structurally positive BTC VRP and recommend long vol only tactically (cheap absolute IV, pre-catalyst). Indian Delta Exchange content is dominated by short-straddle/theta algos. Fee caps make Delta cheaper than Deribit for low-premium short-dated options.

### Cited Findings
- **Deribit fees:** 0.03% of underlying per option (maker and taker), capped at **12.5% of the option price**. That is the loosest cap in the market, against Delta at 3.5% and OKX at 7%. — [BitDegree Deribit review](https://www.bitdegree.org/crypto/deribit-review); [Spark exchange comparison](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-options-exchange-comparison); [Deribit fee-cut announcement](https://insights.deribit.com/exchange-updates/deribit-lowers-option-trading-fees-to-make-the-market-more-accessible-to-retail-traders/)
- **Delta Exchange (Global):** Options cost 0.010% of notional, capped at **3.5% of premium**, for both maker and taker. — [Delta Global fee support article](https://global.delta.exchange/support/solutions/articles/80001140285-fees-on-options-and-futures-trading); [Cryptodium review](https://cryptodium.org/blog/delta-exchange-crypto-options-trading-fees-review-are-they-really-the-lowest-in-the-market/)
- **Delta Exchange India fee page:** Futures cost 0.02% maker and 0.05% taker. The options cap is shown as "3.5% 10% of Option Premium" (ambiguous rendering, possibly a strike-through change from 10% to 3.5%). **18% GST applies on fees.** Taker fees are also charged on open contracts at settlement. — [delta.exchange/fees](https://www.delta.exchange/fees)
- **Tony Stewart** (ex-Morgan Stanley BTC vol desk head) writes Deribit's "Option Flows" commentary on gamma buying and fast-money daily put buying. — [Deribit Option Flows](https://insights.deribit.com/option-flows/); [Option Flow: Scrambling for Gamma](https://insights.deribit.com/option-flows/option-flow-scrambling-for-gamma/)
- **Block Scholes** publishes monthly volatility reviews and Bybit analytics. Its Aug 2026 note on record-low short-dated IV is cited in section 1. — [Block Scholes research](https://www.blockscholes.com/research)
- **QCP Capital** notes are mostly macro/positioning (e.g. low-liquidity weekend spikes that retrace when markets reopen, long-dated call buying). No explicit systematic long-straddle recommendations were found. — [Yahoo/QCP holiday liquidity](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bitcoin-trapped-until-2026-holiday-140731041.html) (aggregator)
- **Amberdata (Samneet Chepal, CFA):** see section 3. Amberdata also stresses that IV is "solved-for, not forecast". — [Amberdata blog](https://blog.amberdata.io/implied-volatility-reading-the-markets-forecast)

### Inferences
- On Deribit, a cheap 0DTE OTM strangle leg (say $20 premium) can pay up to 12.5% of premium per side in fees, roughly 25% round-trip plus spread if closed rather than expired. On Delta, the 3.5% cap (plus GST, so about 4.1%) is far kinder. This is a big deal for long-OTM-strangle buyers.
- Holding to expiry avoids the exit fee and spread, but Delta India charges a settlement taker fee on open contracts.

### Gaps
- Greeks.live, Laevitas, Paradigm, Glassnode and Genesis Volatility content on *long*-vol rules was not found or not fetched within the budget.
- No Indian YouTube or Telegram long-straddle BTC daily content with verifiable results was found. The Delta India options fee rate on notional was not confirmed from the fetched page.
